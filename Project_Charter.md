Project Charter
Project Title
University Website Management System

Project Overview
The University Website Management System is a web-based platform designed to provide students, faculty members, administrators, and visitors with easy access to university-related information and services. The system serves as a centralized portal for academic information, course details, announcements, events, faculty profiles, and student resources.

The platform aims to improve communication, streamline administrative processes, and enhance the overall user experience by providing an accessible and user-friendly digital environment.

Project Purpose
The purpose of this project is to develop a modern, responsive, and secure university website that enables effective communication between the university and its stakeholders while providing essential academic and administrative services online.

Project Goals and Objectives
Primary Goals
Develop a responsive and user-friendly university website.
Provide centralized access to academic and administrative information.
Improve communication between students, faculty, and administration.
Ensure secure user authentication and authorization.
Enhance accessibility of university services and resources.
Objectives
Allow users to access university information online.
Enable administrators to manage website content efficiently.
Provide students with access to course-related information.
Allow faculty members to manage and update their profiles.
Publish announcements, news, and upcoming events.
Provide a secure and scalable platform for future enhancements.
